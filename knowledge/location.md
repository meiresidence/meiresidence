## What the area actually is

- Qerret sits at the **southern end of the Golem strip**, between Golem and the
  town of Kavaje. Golden sand, shallow water, and a belt of **mature pine forest**
  between the road and the beach.
- The strip is a **developed seasonal resort area**: hotels, guesthouses, beach
  bars, restaurants, cafes and minimarkets along the Durres-Kavaje coastal road,
  plus a long seafront promenade running several kilometres through Golem.
- It is **busy from May to September and much quieter in winter** — say this
  plainly if a client asks; it is the normal rhythm of Albanian coastal rentals
  and it is already priced into how the Ramada programme is run.
- **Shkembi i Kavajes (Kavaja Rock)** marks the northern end of the strip and is
  the landmark locals use for the area.
- The residence is **not isolated** — but it is also not in a city. A client who
  wants city life every evening should hear that Durres is ~20 minutes away.

## Getting there

- **From Tirana Airport:** ~40 min by car, taxi or transfer — Rinas to the
  Tirana-Durres highway, then south on the coastal road.
- **From Tirana centre:** A3 / highway to Durres, then the **SH4 Durres-Kavaje
  coastal road** south through Golem to Qerret. ~45 min.
- **From Durres:** ~16 km straight down the coastal road, ~20 min.
- **By bus:** the Tirana-Durres-Kavaje line runs along the coastal road and stops
  on the strip; Durres to Qerret is roughly 15-20 minutes.
- Ferries from Italy (Bari, Ancona) dock at **Durres port**, ~20 min away — worth
  mentioning to Italian buyers.
