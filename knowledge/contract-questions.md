# Contract, ownership, money and the 6% — how to answer

_Added 2026-08-22 after a live client asked 20 concrete questions (contracts, what
the 6% is calculated on, yearly costs, taxes, dates, penalties, what happens if the
brand leaves) and got nothing but "a colleague will reply to you personally very
soon." That reply is a failure. Every question below has an answer the agent gives
itself, and — where a fact is genuinely not settled yet — a SPECIFIC named next
step instead of a brush-off._

## The rule for this whole section

1. **Answer the part you can, first, with real numbers.** Never open with a deferral.
2. **Do the arithmetic yourself.** 5% of a price, 6% of a price, 6% over 5 years —
   these are not "questions for the team", they are multiplication. Show the figure.
3. **Where the written term is not settled below, say what IS true, then name the
   exact open item and who closes it** — "Eglenti t'i kalon të gjitha pikat e
   kontratës me ty para rezervimit" — never "dikush do të të kontaktojë".
4. **Never invent a clause, a penalty, a tax, a fee or a percentage.** If it is not
   written here or in the knowledge base, it does not exist for you.
5. Legal and tax questions get facts, never advice. No "you should", no "it's a good
   time to buy for tax reasons".

## Money arithmetic — always compute, never defer

Worked examples (use the client's own figure, not these):

| Price | 5% to reserve | 6% per year | 6% over 5 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| 103,500 EUR | 5,175 EUR | 6,210 EUR | 31,050 EUR |
| 171,000 EUR | 8,550 EUR | 10,260 EUR | 51,300 EUR |
| 205,000 EUR | 10,250 EUR | 12,300 EUR | 61,500 EUR |

Phrase it plainly: *"Për 171,000 €, 6% e garantuar do të thotë 10,260 € në vit —
rreth 855 € në muaj — dhe 51,300 € gjatë 5 viteve."*
The 6% is calculated **on the investment amount — the price of the apartment you
pay** — not on a rental figure, not on a valuation. Say that when asked.

## Settled — the agent answers these itself

- **The two programs (pick ONE, never both, never added together):**
  (a) Ramada Residences rental pool, 5+5 years, net profit split **65% owner /
  35% SPV** (the management company). (b) **6% guaranteed** on the investment
  amount, for the first **5 years** (a 10-year version exists too).
- **Is the 6% tied to how much the apartment is rented?** No — it is a fixed
  contractual amount, not a share of the nights sold; that is what "e garantuar"
  means. The exact wording is in the contract, which Eglent sends before signing.
- **Who owns the apartment?** The buyer. In BOTH programs the buyer receives the
  **Property Deed from the Albanian Property Registry (kadastra) as sole legal
  owner** — the management company only manages the rental.
- **Owner use:** 1 week–10 days in high season plus 2–3 weeks off season, free, for
  the owner or their family.
- **Who pays for maintenance, cleaning, bookings, marketing?** The management
  company under Ramada Residences by Wyndham standards. The SPV also covers
  renovation. The owner pays nothing else for at least 10 years.
- **Payment structure:** 5% to reserve (can be done online) → around 50% on signing
  at the public Notary → the remaining 45% in instalments from signing until
  handover in June 2027. A schedule built around the client's own cash flow is the
  one payment question that still goes to Eglent.
- **Dates:** construction completes **Q4 2026**; the residence **opens June 2027**.
  Both are true and they are not a contradiction — the building is finished at the
  end of 2026, then it is furnished, equipped and brought up to Ramada Residences
  by Wyndham operating standards, and it opens at the start of the 2027 season.
  Say exactly that much; do not invent a reason beyond it.
- **Foreign buyers** may buy property in Albania; the process is straightforward.
- **Unit facts** — price, m2, typology, FREE/SOLD/RESERVED, floor (the code is
  letter + floor + number: A114 = block A, floor 1, unit 14), tour link and 3D plan
  — all come from the inventory list. Look them up, never defer them.
- **"How many 2+1 are free? Give me the unit numbers."** Count them in the
  inventory and list the codes with price and m2. That is a lookup, not a handoff.

## Settled 24 Aug 2026 — confirmed by Eglent, answer these directly

- **The three documents.** A buyer signs three: the **sales / ownership contract**,
  the **management contract**, and the **administration contract**. The **6%
  guarantee lives inside the management contract** — it is not a separate document.
- **When the documents are shared:** they go to the buyer **after the 5% reservation
  payment**, not before. Say that warmly and without apology — it is the normal
  order, and Eglent walks a buyer through every point before that step. Never
  promise to email contracts to someone who has not reserved, and never quote or
  summarise a clause you have not been given.
- **The seller is Mei Residence SHPK** — that is the legal entity named in the sales
  contract.
- **When ownership passes:** the buyer becomes the legal owner when the **Property
  Certificate (Certifikata e Pronësisë) is registered in their name** — that is the
  moment, not the earlier registration of the contract in the kadastra.
- **Is the 6% gross or net?** It is **net from Mei Residence's side** — nothing is
  deducted by us before it reaches the owner. Whatever tax applies in the owner's own
  country of residence depends on that country's law, and we do not advise on it:
  say that plainly and let them check with their own accountant. **Paid once a year.**
- **What the owner pays each year:** only the **annual property tax**. The old
  **~0.6 EUR/m2 monthly administration fee no longer applies** — never quote it.
- **Late instalment:** a penalty of **0.1% per day of delay** on the late amount.
  State it plainly if asked; do not soften it and do not add conditions of your own.
  (0.1%/day on a 10,000 EUR instalment is 10 EUR a day.)

## Settled 9 Sep 2026 — the guarantee's own terms, confirmed by Eglent

_From Eglent's answers to a broker's 11-point list about the 6% (the Ania thread,
9 Sep 2026). These close most of what was open above. Answer them directly._

- **When the 6% starts accruing:** **end of June 2027** — the opening. Not the date
  the price is fully paid, not the handover. So the first annual payment falls due a
  year after that. Say the start date plainly whenever someone asks what they get
  "from day one" — the honest answer is that it runs from the opening.
- **Force majeure — the one stated exclusion.** In a force-majeure event the **6% is
  not paid**. This is Eglent's own wording and the agent states it the same way: not
  buried, not softened, and not expanded either. It is the ONLY exclusion you may
  name. If they ask what else could suspend or reduce it, say that force majeure is
  the exclusion you can state and that the rest of the conditions are written in the
  management contract, which Eglent goes through with them personally.
- **Who is legally responsible for paying the 6%:** **Mei Residence SHPK**, the
  seller entity, under the management contract. **Never say Wyndham, Ramada, or "the
  hotel operator" guarantees it** — the brand runs the operation, it does not carry
  the payment obligation. This is the question serious investors care most about, so
  get the entity right.
- **The 10-year version does not step down.** If it runs the full 10 years the rate
  stays **fixed at 6%** throughout — it does not taper after year 5.
- **Is the 5% reservation fee refundable?** Only inside the agreed window: the client
  and Mei agree a period of **2–3 weeks** in which the client comes to sign the
  agreement, and the 5% is refundable during that period. Outside it, it is not. Say
  exactly that — do not present the 5% as freely refundable, and do not present it as
  non-refundable either.
- **Can the apartment be sold while it is in the programme?** **Yes** — and the new
  buyer **must also sign the management contract**. The programme follows the
  apartment; it is not something the seller can strip out of it.
- **Can they switch between the two programmes?** **Yes, but only after 5 years** —
  6% guaranteed → 65/35 rental pool, and 65/35 → 6% guaranteed, the same rule both
  ways. Not before.
- **How the free owner stay actually works:** the owner **books it like any other
  guest** — same reservation process — at a **100% discount**. Up to **10 days in
  summer, August excluded**, plus **2–3 weeks between November and April**. August is
  peak and is not part of the free stay. The 6% is still paid in full for that year
  (settled 30 Aug, unchanged).

### Contract templates before reserving — the one thing that changed

The rule above still stands as the normal order: the three contracts go to the buyer
**after** the 5% reservation. But a buyer who asks to have **anonymised template
agreements** reviewed by their own lawyer before committing 5% is asking something
reasonable, and Eglent does not refuse it — he routes it to **Mei's lawyer**, and he
asks for the requesting lawyer's or client's **email address** to send them to.

So: never refuse this request, and never promise the documents yourself. Say the
request goes to Eglent and Mei's lawyer, ask for the email address they should go to,
and answer every substantive question they asked in the same reply. Do not attach,
quote, summarise or characterise any clause you have not been given.

## Not settled yet — say this, and name the next step

Never answer these with silence or with "someone will contact you". Give the honest
line, then the named step. Keep it to 2–3 lines.

- **Is there a management fee on top, in either program** → the owner's yearly cost
  is the property tax (above); anything beyond that is set out in the management
  contract, which Eglent goes through before the reservation. Do not invent a fee and
  do not promise there is none beyond what is written above.

- **What happens if the company does not pay the 6%** → partly settled now: the
  obligation sits with **Mei Residence SHPK** under the management contract, and
  **force majeure** is the one stated case in which the 6% is not paid (both above).
  Beyond that, do not speculate and do not reassure with invented remedies: say the
  remaining conditions and remedies are written in the management contract and that
  Eglent goes through exactly that part with them personally before any reservation.
- **Taxes and fees outside the apartment price** — notary, kadastra registration,
  transfer tax, VAT, infrastructure tax → not confirmed yet. Say the yearly cost to
  the owner is the property tax (above), that any one-off costs at signing are
  itemised by Eglent before the reservation, and give no figure of your own.
- **Does Ramada / Wyndham stay after 2027, and what if the brand leaves** → the
  management contract runs 5+5 years under Ramada Residences by Wyndham. What
  happens on a brand change is a contract question for Eglent. The one thing that
  is certain and worth saying: the buyer owns the property outright, with the deed
  in their own name, whatever happens to the management arrangement.
- **Orientation / view of a specific unit** → the inventory has floor and typology
  but no orientation column. Say so plainly in one clause and offer the 3D plan and
  video tour, which show the layout, plus Eglent for the exact orientation.
- **When was this price last updated** → say the price list is the current sales
  list and that Eglent confirms today's figure in writing before any reservation.
  Never invent a date.

When a client sends a list like the one below, walk it in their order, one short

4. the exact 6% formula → fixed contractual amount on the price paid, not a share of nights
5. gross or net → net from Mei's side, paid once a year; tax in the owner's own country is theirs
6. what it is calculated on → the investment amount, i.e. the apartment price the buyer pays

11. operator commission → in the rental pool 65% owner / 35% SPV; in the 6% programme nothing is deducted from the 6%
12. taxes / furnishing → property tax yearly; one-off costs at signing itemised by Eglent; furnishing quoted per unit (A212 = +10,400 EUR)

15. when the 6% starts → end of June 2027, the opening
16. what can stop the 6% → force majeure is the one stated exclusion
17. who legally owes the 6% → Mei Residence SHPK, not Wyndham and not the operator
18. is the 5% refundable → only inside the agreed 2–3 week window to come and sign
19. selling while in the programme → allowed; the new buyer signs the management contract
20. switching programme → only after 5 years, either direction
21. templates before reserving → routed to Eglent and Mei's lawyer; ask for the email

Only after all these lines does the meeting or viewing get mentioned — and if
the client asked for one, offer it warmly and escalate.

## What the client should feel at the end of these answers

Concrete, unhurried, and that nothing is being hidden: the numbers are real, the
documents exist and get sent, and the one person who signs off is named. A buyer
asking these questions is the most serious kind of lead we get — they are reading
the fine print. Answer like it.
