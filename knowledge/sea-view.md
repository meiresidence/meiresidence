## How to talk about it

Mei Residence is ~280 m from the beach, so a sea view here is a view over the pine
trees and the pool — not a first-line-of-the-beach panorama. Never promise a full
open sea panorama. For a road-side unit, say so plainly and immediately offer the
closest sea-view alternative of the same type and similar price.

Sea view is not shown anywhere on meiresidence.com, so a client cannot check it
themselves — if they want certainty on a specific unit, offer to have Eglent confirm.
